### 🥉 [Trustify](https://github.com/plc50/Trustify)

**3rd place · Blockchain for Good: Proof of Aid** · Cátedra FinTech, Universidad de Málaga · Sep 2026

Every handover of humanitarian aid becomes a record signed by both hands, chained on Ethereum and paid only on proof, so a donor can check where the aid went.

**My part:** backend owner. The custody API: Sign-In with Ethereum, proof of proximity, encrypted write-once evidence, notary co-signature and relayer.

`Solidity` `TypeScript` `Fastify` `PostgreSQL` `EIP-712` `Docker`

**4th of 12 · Mercedes-Benz AI FinOps hackathon** · Universidad de Málaga · Jul 2026

An OpenAI-compatible proxy that keeps every AI request under budget: it sends each call to the cheapest model that can do the job and warns, degrades or blocks before a team overspends.

**My part:** the proxy core (routing, budgets, degradation, reports and tests), the React console and the one-command demo.
